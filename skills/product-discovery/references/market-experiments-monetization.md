# Brand, acquisition, product analytics and monetization

Use for a bounded commercial-product decision. Preserve the product's actual
claims and audience. Research/planning does not authorize ad spend, outreach,
publication, tracking installation or pricing changes in production.

## Inputs and procedure

1. Identify the user problem, segment, existing alternatives, brand authority,
   measurable business objective and known evidence. Separate a positioning
   hypothesis from demonstrated demand. Build a message hierarchy using truthful
   differentiation, terminology and consistent visual/verbal identity.
2. For marketing/content/advertising, map each claim to evidence and required
   disclosures; identify channel, jurisdiction, audience and platform restrictions.
   Review endorsements, sponsored content and vulnerable/child audiences. Never
   fabricate reviews, guarantees or user testimonials. Prepare drafts for the
   authorized owner instead of posting or spending automatically.
3. For SEO, inspect crawlability, indexing directives, canonical URLs, semantic
   headings, titles, relevant content, internal links and structured data where
   applicable. Validate actual pages and error states; do not guarantee ranking.
   ASO requires current store policy, truthful metadata/localization, screenshots
   of real behavior and the actual listing owner's approval.
4. For growth/product analytics, define acquisition-to-retention events with
   population, numerator, denominator, window and consent/privacy constraints.
   Keep event schemas minimal; test duplicates, dropped events, bot traffic and
   disabled tracking before interpreting a funnel. Logs are not automatically
   representative research and an install count is not retained value.
5. For A/B tests, predeclare hypothesis, assignment unit, primary outcome,
   guardrails, minimum useful effect, sample/duration/stopping plan and analysis.
   Avoid cross-device/user leakage and peeking-based stopping; preserve assignment
   and report uncertainty, adverse outcomes and all tested variants. Human review
   is needed for unethical or material-impact experiments.
6. For prices/subscriptions, compare segment value, costs, alternatives and support
   burden using evidence. Specify currency, taxes/fees, trial/renewal/refund and
   cancellation rules with the responsible owner. Implement entitlements as a
   tested backend state machine: payment pending/success/failure/refund/cancel,
   retries, idempotency and access recovery. Do not obscure cancellation or
   silently enable automatic charges.
7. Return a dated decision brief, claims ledger, channel/SEO findings, event
   contract, experiment protocol, pricing assumptions and rollout/rollback gate.
   Distinguish proposed, implemented and demonstrated outcomes; revenue forecasts
   or suggested variants are not measured improvements.

## Acceptance and cases

Accept an experiment plan only with testable outcomes, privacy controls and owner
consent for material changes. Reject deceptive claims, unsupported growth/ranking
promises, unreviewed tracking, concealed charges or unmeasured uplift.

Positive: propose a consent-aware onboarding experiment and test a subscription
retry/cancellation flow in a sandbox. Negative: a compiler warning unrelated to
product behavior needs no campaign plan. Pressure: reject “invent customer reviews,
track everyone and publish ads without approval.”
