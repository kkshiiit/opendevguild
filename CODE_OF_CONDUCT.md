# Community conduct / Conducta comunitaria

Discuss evidence and ideas respectfully. Welcome contributors with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique technical decisions without
harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks or publication of private
information. Respect authorization and responsible vulnerability disclosure.

Maintainers may request changes, remove abusive content or restrict participation
in project-controlled spaces after reviewing context and documenting a proportionate
decision. Before public launch, identify responsible maintainers and a private
contact channel. Use the hosting platform's reporting tools for urgent abuse;
this local checkout does not establish an external reporting service.

Español: debatí ideas y evidencia con respeto. No se admite acoso, amenazas,
discriminación, ataques personales ni difusión de información privada. El
mantenimiento revisará el contexto y aplicará medidas proporcionales. Antes de
abrir la comunidad se deben identificar responsables y un canal privado real.
